# Support

Start with [installation](docs/INSTALL.md) and [troubleshooting](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

When requesting help, include:

- Reminderz watch and Connector versions;
- Pebble model/platform and mobile app version;
- macOS and Tailscale versions;
- which Connector check is not green; and
- the exact watch error, with reminder titles and private addresses removed.

Never share the pairing payload, QR URL, access token, private Tailscale hostname, or screenshots of
personal reminders. Normal bugs belong in the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/GeezusChrotch/reminderz/issues),
and security-sensitive reports belong in a [private Security Advisory](https://github.com/GeezusChrotch/reminderz/security/advisories/new).

Reminderz is an independent community project; Apple, Tailscale, Pebble, and Rebble do not provide
support for it. It is free, MIT-licensed community software and does not include paid support or a
service-level guarantee.
